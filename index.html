Kite Runner book review

My review of this book is not like anything else I have ever read before in my life. This book changed my whole perspective on how I see books and will look at them ever again in the future. I don’t usually like reading because I don’t find it interesting and worth it to flip those pages, but what made this book especially enjoyable to me is how it didn’t accept the concept of what's wrong. Instead of ignoring wrong actions, it makes us think about them and makes us understand it's wrong by hearing it ourselves. That way this detail was put in really stood out to me. Another thing that made the book special to me was that it had included some non-English words. Since I know a bit of another language, that part made it feel more personal to me never had a book been like that. For example in this book, the phrase baba means father, and bazaar means store. Little details like those in the book make me more curious and more curious to keep on reading. I wanted to find out what other non-English words I might understand but sadly another language doesn't mean the language used in Afghanistan. Overall, this book helped me just gain a new perspective in reading and made me realize that reading can actually be meaningful and fun when the book feels more tailored to you and makes you think and connect with it like never before. It didn’t just make my assignment possible, it made me reflect on life more than I usually do and makes me want to just be a better person. This book truly has forever changed how I feel about reading, and I will definitely look at books with a different perspective from now on. .
